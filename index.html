Andrea Toscano

Incident response & threat hunting at Deloitte, Turin

I work in Deloitte’s Crisis Management & Incident Response team on threat hunting, incident response and digital forensics for enterprise environments. My work runs from hypothesis-driven hunts mapped to MITRE ATT&CK to triage, containment and forensic reconstruction of attacker activity on Windows and Linux.

My offensive security background comes from six years of CTF competitions and the OSCP+, and it shapes how I design hunting hypotheses and detections.
